# Video test clips

Copy the AVI files in this directory to `/video` on the TF card.

All clips were converted to the video format supported by MageBox:

- AVI container
- MJPEG video
- 240x240 resolution
- 30 fps
- `Caminandes_AUDIO_TEST_PCM.avi` keeps a 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo PCM audio track
- the other clips have no audio stream
- short clips are 10 seconds; files ending in `_Full` keep the full duration

Sources:

- `Big_Buck_Bunny.avi`: Blender Foundation, [Big Buck Bunny extract](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Big_Buck_Bunny_extract.ogv), CC BY 3.0.
- `Big_Buck_Bunny_Full.avi`: complete 9:56 version from the [Blender official download archive](https://download.blender.org/peach/bigbuckbunny_movies/), CC BY 3.0.
- `Sintel.avi`: Blender Foundation, [Sintel excerpt](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sintel_excerpt.ogv), CC BY 3.0.
- `Caminandes.avi`: 10-second test clip from Blender Foundation's [Caminandes: Gran Dillama](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Caminandes_-_Gran_Dillama_-_Blender_Foundation%27s_new_Open_Movie.webm), CC BY-SA 3.0.
- `Caminandes_AUDIO_TEST_PCM.avi`: 10-second audio test from the same source; use it to verify PCM playback and noise.
- `Caminandes_Full.avi`: complete 2:26 version of the same film, CC BY-SA 3.0.
